CHINARMIST
Website Development Brief
Primary Objective: Build a modern, premium and minimalist website focused primarily on customized/branded
water bottles, with regular mineral water presented as a secondary offering.
1. Website Purpose
• Create a professional website for CHINARMIST specializing in customized/branded water bottles for
businesses, events, hotels, restaurants, weddings, corporate functions and special occasions.
• Main priority: Customized Water Bottles must be the visual and commercial focus of the entire website.
2. Website Style
• Look & feel: Modern, premium, minimalist, clean, trustworthy and water-inspired.
• Avoid: overcrowded sections, excessive colors, cheap-looking graphics and unnecessary animations.
• Use generous white space, strong visual hierarchy and a fully responsive mobile-first layout.
3. Homepage Hero
• Heading: Customized Water Bottles for Your Brand
• Supporting text: Premium-quality bottled water customized with your logo, branding and design — perfect for
businesses, events, hotels, restaurants and special occasions.
• Primary CTA: Customize Your Bottle
• Secondary CTA: Request a Quote
• Hero imagery should show premium bottles with different customized labels/logos.
4. Customized Water Bottles — Main Section
• Heading: Your Brand. Your Bottle. Your Identity.
• Explain that CHINARMIST provides customized bottles according to the customer's branding requirements.
• Categories: Restaurant Bottles, Hotel Bottles, Corporate Bottles, Wedding Bottles, Event Bottles, Cafe Bottles,
School/University Bottles and Promotional Bottles.
• Use attractive, realistic bottle mockups.
5. Customization Process
• 01 — Share Your Design: Customer sends logo, brand name or preferred design.
• 02 — Choose Your Bottle: Select bottle size, style and packaging requirements.
• 03 — Approve the Design: Prepare a label/design preview for approval.
• 04 — Get Your Customized Bottles: Produce and deliver according to agreed requirements.
6. Bottle Options
• Show editable bottle sizes such as 250ml, 330ml, 500ml and 1.5L.
• Do not hard-code availability. Sizes, MOQ, product images and quote settings must be editable from the
admin/CMS.
• Each product should support: bottle image, size, customization availability, minimum order quantity and Request
Quote button.
7. Custom Label / Branding
• Highlight customization with: Company Logo, Brand Name, Colors, Custom Label, Event Name, Wedding
Names, Restaurant Branding, Hotel Branding, Corporate Branding and Special Messages.
8. Regular Mineral Water — Secondary
• Create a secondary section titled Premium Mineral Water.
• Present regular mineral water without letting it compete visually with customized bottles.
• CTA: View Mineral Water.
9. Why Choose CHINARMIST
• Premium Quality — Quality-focused bottled water and professional packaging.
• Custom Branding — Put your logo, colors and identity on every bottle.
• Professional Presentation — Make your business or event look more premium.
• Flexible Orders — Options for different bottle sizes and requirements.
• Business & Events — Suitable for restaurants, hotels, offices, weddings and events.
• Only use claims that CHINARMIST can actually verify.
10. Industries / Customers
• Show: Restaurants, Hotels, Cafes, Corporate Offices, Weddings, Events, Conferences, Parties, Schools &
Universities and Promotional Campaigns.
• CTA: Create Your Custom Bottle.
11. Gallery
• Create a premium gallery for customized bottles, label designs, restaurant/hotel bottles, event bottles, corporate
bottles and packaging.
• Gallery images must be easy to update from the admin panel.
12. Request a Quote
• Customer: Name, Company/Organization, Phone/WhatsApp, Email, City.
• Order: Bottle Size, Estimated Quantity, Customization Required, Delivery Location, Additional Requirements.
• File Upload: Logo, label design, reference image or branding file.
• CTA: Get My Quote.
• Show a clear success message after submission.
13. WhatsApp CTA
• Add a prominent Chat on WhatsApp button throughout the website. WhatsApp number must be editable from
admin/settings.
14. Navigation
• Keep navigation simple: Home | Customized Bottles | Mineral Water | How It Works | Gallery | About Us |
Contact
• Add a highlighted Get a Quote button.
• Use a clean hamburger menu on mobile.
15. About CHINARMIST
• Use concise copy explaining that CHINARMIST provides bottled water solutions with a strong focus on
customized water bottles, helping businesses and event organizers present their brand professionally through
personalized bottle packaging.
• Do not add fake history, certifications, awards or statistics. Keep factual content editable.
16. Design System
• Colors: White, deep/navy blue, soft blue, very light blue and optional subtle aqua accent.
• Typography: Inter, Manrope or Plus Jakarta Sans.
• Use bold/semibold headings, clean body text, generous spacing and strong CTA hierarchy.
17. Animations
• Use subtle fade-ins, bottle image reveals, hover effects, smooth scrolling and button micro-interactions. Avoid
excessive animation that slows the website.
18. Mobile Design
• Fully responsive: Mobile → Tablet → Desktop.
• On mobile, keep the hero strong, CTAs easy to tap, WhatsApp easily accessible, product cards properly stacked
and the quote form simple.
19. SEO
• Optimize for: Customized water bottles, Custom water bottles, Branded water bottles, Personalized water
bottles, Custom bottled water, Customized mineral water bottles, Corporate water bottles, Wedding water
bottles and Restaurant branded water bottles.
• Use proper page titles, meta descriptions, H1/H2 structure, image alt text, clean URLs and schema where
appropriate.
20. Admin / CMS Requirements
• Make bottle products, bottle sizes, prices/Request Quote, MOQ, gallery images, customized examples,
testimonials, contact information, WhatsApp number, FAQs and About Us content editable without code.
21. Critical Business Priority
Customized Water Bottles = Primary Product
Regular Mineral Water = Secondary Product
• The website's visual hierarchy, homepage, CTAs, SEO and navigation must reflect this priority.
• Within the first 5–10 seconds, visitors should understand that CHINARMIST specializes in customized branded
water bottles.
• The overall website should feel like a premium B2B/custom packaging brand, not simply a generic mineral-water
website.
Main Brand Message
• CHINARMIST — Water, Customized for Your Brand.
